## Design decisions and the reasoning

**A Severe cut should be felt, not heard as a fourth kind of cut.** 3-4 tiles is the most common cut
in real play, so giving it Big's treatment would spend the loudest effect on the ordinary case. It
therefore borrows Big's *weight* (stop, shiver, zoom, thud, haptic) at roughly half amplitude, and
adds one new signifier — the seam flash and the neighbours' flinch — that says "this is a cut along
your edge", which Medium cannot say. 5+ keeps Big/Huge exactly as they were so the top of the scale
does not drift.

**The enemy's body must not move the piles.** The deck and both piles are information (counts, tops);
moving them while a card travels would make the count lie for ~300 ms. So the flyer is a separate
element and `show()` — which repaints the piles — runs on arrival. The flyer is inside `#game` under
the tutorial glass (z 34) and lesson plate (z 42) and is `pointer-events:none`, because the lesson
must never lose a lit target or a tap to decoration.

**The press is a state, not an animation.** Phase 0 puts `data-pressed` and `--press-depth` on the
element for as long as the pointer is down, so the paint is synchronous on `pointerdown` (0.1 ms) and
survives a slow frame; the visual is CSS, therefore it also has a designed static state under Reduce
motion. The press primitive deliberately fires no action: the existing click path stays the only
thing that mutates the game.

**One scheduler, two tempos.** Rather than retune every surface, `ack`/`consequence` were chosen at
the values the product already played (96/336) and the legacy names alias them, so the tokens can be
reviewed without a visual diff. The pinned choreography that must not drift is asserted in
`web/tests/motion.test.ts`.

## Not done (deliberately)

Phases 1-5 of the wider feel plan (tap feedback everywhere, the angled bloom, the sound bank, the
breathing hand, the composed first frame) were **not started**. Phase 1's groundwork is already in
the branch (every surface paints the press), but its card-weight-by-rank half is not. Starting a
phase that could not be finished green inside the window would have left `/3/` red or half-changed,
which the brief forbids. `/3/` is live and testable at **762ce68**; the per-phase work is the next
sitting.

## Follow-up, same day: smoother, lighter, and the chain reaction (branch `futa05-smooth-2026-10-20`)

Measured first, on a phone-sized window with a 4x slower CPU and slow 4G (local build, level 1):

| Change | Commit | Result |
|---|---|---|
| The lit move is worked out once per position (cache by immutable engine state); the hand asks for the lit card once per redraw instead of once per card | `c5398b9` | full redraws ~20% cheaper (p90 ~143 → ~110 ms at 4x CPU); nothing shown changes |
| Stone counters re-encoded (WebP q100) and gold frames as lossless WebP | `cd0d93f` | 1,166 → 515 KB and 637 → 425 KB; rendered at game size: mean difference 0.3/255 (stones), gold pixel-identical |
| Thrown card flies 320 ms with its apex at .48 again (`PINNED.throwFlight`), the step's 396 ms wait unchanged | `c2ca487` | `762ce68` had silently undone `32359b5`; `futa04-motion.ts` passes its throw check again |
| **The chain reaction**: when a cut of 3+ of their tiles ripples outward, each ring lands with a small wooden pop a whole tone above the last (max 6), and a 7 ms tick on phones that vibrate — only after the cut's own buzz has finished | `46a9335` | unit tests (plan, sound, haptic) + `cut-severe.ts` (no pops on 1-2 tiles or Reduce motion, rising pops on 3/4/8) |

What was checked and **not** changed, and why:

- **Stutters on taps are style/layout work spread across the whole redraw**, not image decoding.
  Removing every `:has()` rule changed nothing measurable, and caching the window size only moved
  the forced layout to the next read. A real fix means restructuring `render()` into a read phase
  then a write phase — a larger change that was not worth the risk here.
- **Online code still loads in the background** after the menu is usable. Deferring it would change
  when online styles apply (the fenced-off online area), for no first-screen gain.
- `futa04-motion.ts`'s "Mega Bomb under 700 ms" is a wall-clock check that is borderline in a slow
  container on the untouched live commit too (720-856 ms there, 789-824 ms with these changes).
