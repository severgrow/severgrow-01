    How to play
    • You are green and start from the root marked R at the bottom left. The bot is purple.
    • Each turn: draw a card, play any card combos, discard one card, then Continue (or Knock).
    • Hypha = 3+ cards of one suit in a row (like Moss 3-4-5). It grows a straight line of tiles from next to your network, strength rising outward.
    • Bloom = 3-4 cards of the same number in different suits. It grows a small clump of tiles, all that strength.
    • A new tile can replace a bot tile only if it is stronger. Roots can never be replaced.
    • Tiles that lose their path back to their root wither (Sever). Cut the bot's links, protect yours.
    • Leftover cards that are not in combos are deadwood. Too much deadwood when you Continue makes your weakest edge tiles rot.
    • Knock when your deadwood is 10 or less: the bot gets one last turn, then the higher score wins (a tie goes to the bot).
    • Once per game, Fruit: give up 3 of your linked tiles to destroy one touching bot tile, however strong.
    • Surround the bot's root completely (with at least one of your tiles) to win at once: Strangle.
    • Tiles score 1, gold rich hexes score 2. Grey hexes are rock.

    Tip: tap a hex or a card to see only the moves that use it.
